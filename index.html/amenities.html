Quick tips to good camping at O'Connell!

  • Pools can be enjoyed from 10:00 am until 9:00 pm daily, in season. Beach pleasures are from 10:00 am until dusk. NOTE: NO LIFEGUARDS ARE PROVIDED AT THE MOMENT, CHILDREN AGES 15 AND UNDER MUST BE ACCOMPANIED BY AN ADULT.
  • No smoking inside fenced pool areas. Smoking is permitted outside of our pool fenced-in complexes. This includes E-Cigarettes and Vaping.
  • Please be considerate of your fellow campers and keep music & loud noises to a minimum. Likewise, we always observe the quiet hours from 11:00 pm until 7:00 am.
  • TREES ARE TREASURES. Please be kind to trees by not damaging them in any way or tying pets, clotheslines, or other items to them.
