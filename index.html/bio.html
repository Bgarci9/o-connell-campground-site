About the developer

Hi! My name is Brian Garcia.

My family and I have always been deeply connected to nature, consistently choosing destinations that allow us to immerse ourselves in the outdoors. We have been visiting O'Connell's for nearly a decade—long before its recent management change. Through every transition, the campground has stayed true to its natural roots and vibrant community spirit. With activities always happening, it remains an ideal vacation spot for many families, seeking a balance of nature and community.
