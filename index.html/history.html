A trip through time ...

O'Connell's RV Campground in Amboy, Illinois, has long served as a beloved outdoor retreat for midwestern travelers. Established decades ago, the park originally captured the classic charm of family-oriented campin.

For many years, the property operated as a popular "Yogi Bear's Jellystone Park" franchise, creating generations of memories with themed activities and family-focused recreation.

Recently, the campground underwent a major evolution, transitioning into a Thousand Trails managed property. This shift brought crucial modern infrastructure updates, upgraded amenities, and a refreshed operational vision designed to bridge the gap between its nostalgic past and the demands of modern RV travelers.
